# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework and no heavy libraries (user's choice). Mobile-first. Hostable on any static host.

## Users

People in Brazil looking for insurance who want to talk to a real person: families, small business owners and MEIs, self-employed workers, and older adults (60+). Many arrive from Instagram (@almeida.barross) on a phone and want to understand their options quickly and start a conversation on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Almeida Barros is an insurance brokerage (corretora de seguros). The site's job is to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation or a quote request ("Solicite um orçamento sem compromisso"). Success is a visitor who trusts the brokerage enough to send the first message.

## Positioning

Human service with direct access: "Sem robô. Sem fila. Sem estresse." and "Atendimento humano. Acesso direto. Solução de verdade." The people who answer bring more than 40 years of experience in the insurance market (confirmed: this is the family/team's experience, not the age of the current company, which has a recent SUSEP registration). The broker compares options across several partner insurers on the client's behalf.

## Operating Context

- Main conversion channel: WhatsApp (floating button on every screen plus CTAs through the page).
- Contact also by phone, Instagram and in person at the office.
- Clients come back to the broker after contracting; the relationship is ongoing.

## Product Principles

1. A person answers. Every surface should make it obvious that the next step reaches a human, fast.
2. Clarity over jargon. Explain insurance in everyday words.
3. One easy next step. WhatsApp is always one tap away, with a pre-filled message when context allows.
4. Earned trust. Show real credentials (SUSEP, experience) and never invent proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.x AA contrast, visible focus, alt text on images, keyboard-operable widgets, and respect for prefers-reduced-motion. Older adults are a core audience: generous text size and tap targets, plain language.
