# Design System: Almeida Barros – Corretora de Seguros

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The A That Holds the Page"**

The brokerage's logo, a geometric A built from diagonal bands with three horizontal strokes across it, is not a badge parked in the corner. It is the system's structural material. Its legs grow into large navy and sky bands that cross the hero behind the portrait of the person who answers. They shrink into the slanted blue "tarja" under one word of every title, the slanted number plates of the steps, and the stripe texture on card corners, photo backings, placeholder frames and the footer ribbon. Its crossbar becomes the triple-stroke rule that links the steps and marks the values list. Anything new should be traceable back to one of those two parts of the letter.

Deep navy fields bookend the page: the header and hero at the top, the closing band and footer at the bottom. In between, white and pale-ice sections alternate and carry calm, well-spaced reading. Blue is the voice of action, sky marks things on dark, sand gold appears exactly once, and WhatsApp green exists only on the floating button. Type is generous on purpose: 17px body, 48px controls and left-aligned section heads, so an older reader arriving from Instagram on a phone can follow it without effort.

The direction refuses the insurance-category default of a stock family photo over a blue gradient. Fields are flat color. Depth comes from the A bands and soft navy-tinted shadows, never from gradient washes or glassy effects on light surfaces. Motion is one brand gesture, the tarja wiping in under its word as each title enters view, plus quiet rises, so the page feels assembled by hand rather than animated for show.

**Key Characteristics:**
- The logo's A, at three scales: hero bands, title tarjas, 108deg stripe textures.
- Uppercase Montserrat 800 titles, each with exactly one word on a blue parallelogram.
- Deep navy at both ends of the page, with white and ice alternating between.
- Two blues with separate jobs: a vivid one for graphics, an AA-darkened one for actions.
- Single-use accents: gold for the "+40" seal only, green for the floating WhatsApp button only.
- Soft, layered, navy-tinted shadows. Hover lifts a card by 4px.
- Rounded but not bubbly: 12, 14, 16 and 20px radii, with circles reserved for seals and round buttons.

## Colors

A cool, trustworthy navy-and-blue palette, warmed by one handwritten word and one gold seal, with roughly 60% white and ice reading fields, 30% navy fields and 10% blue and sky accents.

### Primary
- **Brokerage Navy** (#1B2A4E): the ink of the whole system. All headings, body text on light backgrounds, the profile panel, the first step plate, selected tabs, the FAQ widget header, and the closing call-to-action band. Also the browser theme color.
- **Night Navy** (#121C36): the deepest field. The fixed header, hero, privacy-page hero and footer. The header shares the hero's tone so the A bands can run up underneath it.

### Secondary
- **Tarja Blue** (#1E88D2): the brand's vivid blue, kept for graphics. Title tarjas, the third step plate, and the blue segment of the footer ribbon. With white text it measures about 3.8:1, so it carries white type only at display size.
- **Action Blue** (#1A76BE): the vivid blue darkened for AA (4.8:1 with white). Every primary button, the "me" chat bubble, icon color inside ice tiles, rating stars, the focus ring on light backgrounds, and the second step plate.
- **Action Blue Pressed** (#155F9C): hover state for primary buttons and strong links.
- **Link Blue** (#1668AB): inline and strong links on white and ice (5.8:1 on white), card "Quero cotar" links, and team WhatsApp links.

### Tertiary
- **Celeste** (#3DB4E8): the mark color on dark backgrounds. The logo's thin legs and middle stroke, check icons, the handwritten "humano", the desktop nav underline, the hashtag, panel labels, and the focus ring inside dark sections. On light backgrounds it appears only as texture: card-corner stripes at 35% opacity and the middle line of the triple strokes.
- **Seal Sand** (#C9B27C): the "+40 anos de experiência" seal, and nowhere else.
- **WhatsApp Green** (#1DA851): the floating WhatsApp button, and nowhere else.

### Neutral
- **White** (#FFFFFF): the main reading field, card and tile surfaces, and the light button on dark.
- **Ice** (#EEF6FC): alternating section background, icon tiles, the rating block, the FAQ conversation area, and the light-button hover.
- **Deep Ice** (#DCE9F5): placeholder-photo and map-placeholder fields, and the carousel scrollbar.
- **Hairline** (#D3E2F0): 1px dividers, card and tile borders, tab and chip outlines, and the dashed step connector on mobile.
- **Slate Ink** (#4A5878): secondary text on light backgrounds (section intros, card copy, roles, meta). 7:1 on white.
- **Mist** (#BFD3EA): secondary text on navy and deep (hero lead, panel copy, footer text, desktop nav links). 9:1 or more.
- **Band Navy** (#22355F): one step above navy, used for the A's navy bands and strokes and the dark segments of the footer ribbon, so they read against deep.

### Named Rules
**The Two Blues Rule.** Tarja Blue is a graphic color and Action Blue is an interactive one. Anything with white text below display size sitting on blue (buttons, chat bubbles) uses Action Blue. Tarja Blue appears behind white text only in titles and the step numerals.

**The Single-Use Color Rule.** Gold belongs to the "+40" seal, and green belongs to the floating WhatsApp button. In-page WhatsApp calls to action are blue primary buttons carrying the WhatsApp glyph, never green.

**The Navy Is Ink Rule.** Text on light backgrounds is navy (#1B2A4E) or slate (#4A5878), never black or neutral grey. Text on dark backgrounds is white or mist.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Montserrat (variable, self-hosted, with sans-serif fallback)
**Body Font:** Inter (variable, self-hosted, with system-ui, Segoe UI and Roboto fallbacks)
**Accent Font:** Caveat 600 (self-hosted, with Segoe Script and cursive fallbacks), one word only

**Character:** A heavy, geometric, all-caps Montserrat speaks in the confident voice of the brand's Instagram posts. Inter keeps the explanations plain and very readable. A single Caveat word adds the human hand the brand promises.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Montserrat 800, clamp(2.2rem → 3.85rem), line-height 1.08, uppercase): the hero H1 only, white on night navy, wrapped with `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Headline** (Montserrat 800, clamp(1.7rem → 2.75rem), line-height 1.16, 0.005em tracking, uppercase): every section title, navy on light backgrounds or white on dark, always carrying one tarja word.
- **Title** (Montserrat 800, clamp(1.4rem → 1.9rem), line-height 1.2): profile panel headings.
- **Title Small** (Montserrat 700, 1.2–1.25rem, line-height 1.3): card and step titles. Team names (1–1.1rem), values headings (1.05rem) and contact headings (0.95rem) use the same weight at smaller sizes.
- **Motto** (Montserrat 700, clamp(1.1rem → 1.35rem), line-height 1.35): the hero's "Atendimento humano. Acesso direto. Solução de verdade." line. The footer motto uses the same face and weight at text size.
- **Body** (Inter 400, 1.0625rem, line-height 1.65): running copy, kept to roughly 32–40rem measures. Card copy sits at 1rem.
- **Body Lead** (Inter 400, 1.125rem, slate): section intros, capped at 40rem. The about-section lead uses 1.2rem at weight 500 in navy.
- **Label** (Montserrat 700, 0.9–1rem, 0.01em tracking): buttons, tabs, the menu toggle, card links and the FAQ launcher. Chips use Inter 600 at 0.875rem.
- **Label Caps** (Montserrat 800, 0.85rem, 0.12em tracking, uppercase): footer column headings. The logotype tagline uses the same treatment at weight 600, 0.6875rem and 0.1em tracking, in celeste.
- **Hand** (Caveat 600, 1.55em of its line, line-height 0.8, rotated −4deg, celeste): the single word "humano" in the hero motto.

### Named Rules
**The One Tarja Rule.** Every section title (and the privacy-page H1) is Montserrat 800 uppercase with exactly one word set on the blue parallelogram. Any punctuation after that word is pulled in by −0.14em so it hugs the tarja.

**The One Hand Rule.** Caveat appears once per page, on one word. It is a signature, not a style.

## Layout

The page uses a single centered container, `min(100% − 2 × gutter, 1200px)`, with a 20px gutter on mobile that widens to 32px from 768px. Sections breathe on a fluid vertical rhythm of clamp(4rem → 7.5rem). Section heads are left-aligned, capped at 46rem, with a fluid gap of 2–3.25rem to their content. The page is mobile-first, and most breakpoints are content-driven rather than a fixed scale (see the sidecar).

- **Hero:** a single column on mobile: title, motto, lead, a full-width 56px CTA, a quiet "Ver como funciona" link, the three promises, and the SUSEP line. The photo frame starts at the fold with the seal overlapping its top-left corner. From 960px it becomes a 1.08fr / 0.92fr split at least `min(100svh, 920px)` tall: copy on the left, vertically centered, and the portrait on the right sitting on the hero's bottom edge, with the A bands behind it extending up under the header.
- **Steps:** a vertical list with a dashed hairline connector on mobile. From 860px it becomes three columns joined by triple strokes.
- **Insurance cards:** one column, then two from 600px and three from 1000px.
- **Profiles:** a tab row (a 2×2 grid below 700px) above a single dark panel. From 900px the panel splits copy and solutions 1.1fr / 0.9fr.
- **About:** copy and photo split 1fr / 1.05fr from 960px. The values list becomes a ruled three-column table. The video and two gallery images form a 2fr / 1fr mosaic.
- **Partners:** a 2-up grid of logo tiles, 4-up from 720px. **Team:** horizontal cards stacked below 480px, a 2-up grid, then 4-up from 960px. **Reviews:** a horizontal scroll-snap track with cards at `min(86%, 380px)`. **Contact:** the list and map stack, then split 0.9fr / 1.1fr from 960px.
- **Floating layer:** the FAQ launcher and WhatsApp button are fixed bottom-right with a 16px safe-area inset. The footer reserves 6.5rem of bottom padding on mobile so they never cover its content.
- **Image ratios:** hero portrait 5:6, about photo 4:3, team portraits 4:5, gallery and video 16:9.

**The Bookend Rule.** Dark fields open and close the page (night navy for the header and hero, brokerage navy for the closing band, night navy for the footer). Between them, white and ice sections alternate, and two light sections never share a background back to back.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is a hybrid. Light surfaces rest on soft, two-part shadows tinted with navy: a tight contact shadow plus a wide drop with negative spread, so cards look lifted off the ice rather than outlined. Secondary light surfaces (review cards, partner tiles, tabs, chips, carousel buttons) use a hairline border instead of a shadow. Dark fields carry no shadow of their own. Their depth comes from the layered A bands, with the hero portrait casting a Deep shadow onto the field. The scrolled header turns translucent (93% night navy with a 14px blur) over the page.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(18, 28, 54, 0.05), 0 6px 16px -6px rgba(27, 42, 78, 0.1)`): insurance cards, team cards, the contact list, the map, and the "them" chat bubble.
- **Raised** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(18, 28, 54, 0.06), 0 18px 36px -12px rgba(27, 42, 78, 0.22)`): card hover, the about photo, the map card, the FAQ launcher, the skip link, and partner-tile hover.
- **Deep** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -18px rgba(8, 14, 30, 0.55)`): the hero portrait, the FAQ panel and the video play disc.
- **Button glow** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -10px rgba(8, 14, 30, 0.55)`): primary buttons only.

### Named Rules
**The Navy-Tinted Shadow Rule.** Shadows on light surfaces are tinted with the brand's navy (rgba 18 28 54 / 27 42 78), soft and offset downward. No neutral grey drops, no hard offset shadows.

**The Lift-on-Hover Rule.** Hover is a small physical lift, never a layout shift. Cards rise 4px and step from Rest to Raised (0.4s, ease-out), buttons rise 1px and press to 98% scale on click, and round buttons grow to 106%.

## Shapes

The form language is softly rounded rectangles, cut through with the logo's slant.

- **Radii:** 12px for small tiles (icon tiles, the menu toggle, solution rows, the FAQ avatar), 14px for controls (buttons, profile tabs, chat bubbles), 16px for cards and containers (insurance and team cards, partner tiles, review cards, the rating block, gallery images), and 20px for large frames (the profile panel, the hero portrait's top corners, the about photo, the video, the contact list, the map, the FAQ panel). Pills (999px) are kept for chips, the FAQ launcher and the "video coming soon" badge. Full circles are kept for the seal, the WhatsApp button, carousel arrows and review avatars.
- **The hero portrait** is rounded only on top (20px 20px 0 0) and sits flush on the hero's bottom edge, as if standing in front of the A.
- **Chat bubbles** square one corner to 4px on the speaker's side, like a speech tail.
- **Parallelograms:** the tarja and the step plates are clip-path parallelograms leaning like the logo's left legs. The tarja is sheared by 0.3em and the step plate by 15px.
- **Stripes:** repeating linear gradients at `--stripe-angle: 108deg` (18° off vertical, matching the logo). Used as a 96px triangular corner on cards (sky, 3px on / 8px off), a fading band on the right 40% of the profile panel, an offset backing block behind the about photo (navy, 18px offset), a 10px footer ribbon (sky / band navy / blue / band navy), and the stripes inside placeholder images.
- **Triple strokes:** three 2px lines at a 5px pitch, navy / sky / navy, in stepped lengths, taken from the logo's crossbar.

### Named Rules
**The Logo Angle Rule.** Every slanted edge in the system (tarjas, step plates, stripes, the A bands, placeholder hatching) leans like the logo's left legs, about 18° off vertical. Never introduce a second slant angle.

**The Triple Stroke Rule.** When a horizontal rule needs to say "brand" rather than "divider", it is three strokes (navy, sky, navy) in stepped lengths, never a single thick bar. Plain separators stay 1px hairlines.

## Components

### Buttons
Confident, rounded and tactile. Each one reads as a promise of a person on the other end.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (14px), Montserrat 700 label, icon gap 0.6rem, icon at 1.3em.
- **Primary:** Action Blue fill with white text and the button glow. Default 48px tall (0.8rem 1.4rem padding). Large is 56px (1rem 1.6rem, 1rem text) for the hero and closing CTAs. Small is 44px (0.6rem 1.05rem) for the header, map and FAQ. Most primaries carry the WhatsApp glyph and open a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** hover darkens to Action Blue Pressed and lifts 1px. Active presses to 98% scale. Focus is a 3px Action Blue outline at 3px offset (celeste inside dark sections).
- **Light:** a white fill with navy text, used on the navy profile panel. Hover changes to ice.
- **Ghost:** a white fill with navy text and a 1.5px inset hairline that turns navy on hover. Used once, for the map's "Como chegar".
- **Quiet link (on dark):** inherits white, weight 600, with a 44px hit area and a mist underline at 45% that turns solid on hover. It pairs with a primary as the secondary action.

### Chips

### Navigation
- **Header:** fixed, 68px tall (80px from 1024px), night navy, white logotype (Montserrat 800 uppercase name with 0.05em tracking, plus a celeste tagline). Once the page scrolls past 12px it turns 93% navy with a 14px blur and a soft drop.

- **Footer:** night navy topped by the 10px stripe ribbon. It has three columns from 860px (brand and legal, quick links, contacts with celeste icons), Label Caps headings, mist links with 36px hit areas that turn white and underline on hover, and fine print below a 10% white hairline.

### Tarja Title (signature)
The brand's post style, turned into the main heading device. One word is wrapped in a parallelogram of Tarja Blue (padding 0 0.32em, inset 0.04em top and 0.02em bottom), and its text turns white. On load the legs rise 48px in sequence (0, 0.08 and 0.16s), the strokes slide in from the left (0.35–0.49s), the photo rises at 0.2s, and the seal stamps in last.

### Icons

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every section title in Montserrat 800 uppercase with exactly one word on the Tarja Blue parallelogram, and let it wipe in once on entry.
- **Do** use Action Blue (#1A76BE) for any button or filled surface carrying white text below display size, darkening to #155F9C on hover.
