# Landing page — Almeida Barros

Scope: single landing page (index.html) plus privacy page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: families, MEIs/small businesses, self-employed, 60+; mostly on phones from Instagram.
Action: start a WhatsApp conversation / request a quote. Proof on hand: SUSEP 202057902, 40+ years of family/team experience. Everything else is a bracketed placeholder (no invented reviews, partners, team, ratings).
Build path: code-led (no image generation available this session).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brand's own "A" (two diagonal bands plus horizontal strokes) is the page's structure. Every section title is uppercase with one word on a blue tarja cut at the logo's leg angle, and the hero photo sits inside the A's bands. Refuses the category default: stock-family hero on a blue gradient over a row of identical icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy #121C36 hero and closing band, white and ice #EEF6FC reading fields (about 60/30/10), azul vivo darkened for AA on actions, celeste for marks on dark, sand-gold only on the "+40 anos" seal. Montserrat 800 uppercase titles, Inter body, one Caveat word ("humano"). 16px radii, soft navy-tinted offset shadows, diagonal-stripe ribbons as dividers and card corners, triple horizontal strokes as rules.

STORY: A person-led brokerage with 40+ years of experience; three plain steps; find your insurance and your profile; meet the people, partners and reviews; tap WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: header with logo and menu button; H1 "SEGUROS COM AMOR, ATENDIMENTO DE [GENTE]" about 2.3rem; lead line with handwritten "humano"; full-width primary CTA "Solicitar orçamento sem compromisso"; "Sem robô. Sem fila. Sem estresse."; photo frame starts at the fold with the gold seal. Desktop 1440: copy left, photo right inside navy and celeste diagonal bands crossing behind at the logo angle, three horizontal strokes, gold round seal overlapping the frame's lower-left; CTA above the fold. Signature interaction: the tarja wipes in once as each title enters view; profile selector tabs in "Você se encaixa".

FORM: Brand world pinned whole by the brief. seed: none (pinned). Quoted: "Use essas diagonais e listras como elemento gráfico sutil (divisores de seção, fundos, detalhes dos cards)" and "HERO: H1 tipo \"Seguros com amor, atendimento de [gente]\" (palavra em tarja azul) ... CTA principal \"Solicitar orçamento sem compromisso\" + selo dourado \"+40 anos\". Foto de atendimento humanizado com as faixas diagonais do logo ao fundo."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
